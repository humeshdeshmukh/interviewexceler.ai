# How to Use Interview Exceler AI

This guide will walk you through the various features of **Interview Exceler AI** and how to make the most of your interview preparation.

## 1. Getting Started

- **Sign Up / Login**: Use your Google account to sign in securely via Supabase Auth.
- **Profile Setup**: After logging in, complete your profile to receive more personalized interview questions.

## 2. Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard is your central hub where you can:

- View your recent interview performance.
- Track your progress over time.
- Access quick links to start new interview sessions.

## 3. Taking an AI Mock Interview

1. **Select a Track**: Click on "Start New Interview" and choose a job role or industry track (e.g., Software Engineer, Product Manager, etc.).
2. **Setup your environment**: Ensure your microphone and camera (optional) are working correctly.
3. **Answering Questions**: The AI will present you with common and technical questions. You can speak your answers or type them.
4. **Real-time Feedback**: As you answer, the AI provides feedback on your content, tone, and confidence.

## 4. Reviewing Feedback

After each interview session, you'll receive a detailed report including:

- **Overall Score**: A summary of your performance.
- **Strengths**: What you did well.
- **Areas for Improvement**: Constructive feedback on how to improve your answers.
- **AI-Suggested Answers**: See how a top-performing candidate might have answered the same questions.

## 5. Billing and Subscription

- **Manage Subscription**: Access your billing settings to upgrade to premium for unlimited interviews and advanced coaching features.
- **Payments**: All transactions are securely handled via Stripe.
